A proper mark for 鍛える

Three directions for the Kitaeru logo, next to today’s seal. Every character in A, B and C is a vector path: nothing depends on a font at runtime, so the mark can’t shift while the page loads and it renders the same everywhere. Straight (0°) in every direction. Exploration only: the app is untouched.

Each direction has a primary 鍛 seal, a small-size cut for 16–32 px (favicon, inline button seal), app icons (any, Apple 180, Android maskable), a vertical 鍛える seal with the Kitaeru wordmark, and light, dark and one-colour sumi renderings. Assets live in docs/brand/a, /b and /c; fonts and licences are in docs/brand/LICENSES.md.

16 px legibility

Browser tabs at real size, then the exact 16 px and 32 px rasters, enlarged six and three times. 鍛 has 17 strokes, so no version resolves every stroke at 16 px (that would need about one pixel per stroke). What matters there is a distinctive silhouette and rhythm; from 32 px the strokes should read.
