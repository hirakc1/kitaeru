### 15. `zhan_zhuang` — PASS WITH CORRECTIONS

- **Names.** 站桩 / 站樁 zhàn zhuāng: CMQG (站桩功) and Wikipedia. 三圆式, 抱球式, 环抱式, 自然式: CMQG. Confirmed.
- **Form.** CMQG and Lyu 2021 (Table 1, Beijing University of Chinese Medicine) are two independent sources. **Corrections:** (1) in 三圆式 the toes turn slightly in (两足尖内扣, "foot circle"), and Lyu agrees ("toes slightly point inside"); the spec's "feet parallel" is the 自然式. Either change the description or disclose parallel feet as Kitaeru's **[adaptation]**. (2) 抱球式 and 环抱式 differ in how much the arms bend, not in height; both are at chest height. (3) 环抱式: palms face in, about two *chi* from the chest (confirmed). (4) 松而不懈 is in CMQG's 少林剑指站桩 section, not in the 三圆式 text. (5) Stance heights: high = knees slightly bent, middle = about 130°, low = about 90°.
- **Dose.** "Start at 15 minutes, build to 60, 2–3 times a day" is confirmed in CMQG as a clinical practice dose; the Kitaeru default is [practice].
- **History.** Wang Xiangzhai 1885–1963, founder of Yiquan and one of the first to teach zhan zhuang publicly: en and zh Wikipedia and Sogou/Baidu Baike (reference sources; history only).
- **Evidence.** Lyu 2021 is a **protocol** (future tense, two practising arms, no non-practice control, no results). Edwards 2023 (270 RCTs; isometric training ranked highest for SBP; wall squat the top submode) is confirmed. Grade C indirect is right.
- Lam Kam Chuen's *The Way of Energy*: not read (only publisher listings). Cite it only once someone reads the page.

### 17. `surya_namaskar` — PASS WITH CORRECTIONS

- **Leg order.** A: "the left (or right) leg"; Kerala: right leg first. Either order is sourced.
- **Rounds.** "Start with four rounds and build up to twelve" confirmed (A).
- **Devanagari.** सूर्यनमस्कार (Wikipedia) and both सूर्य नमस्कार and सूर्यनमस्कार (The Art of Living, Hindi). Either spelling is attested; the spacing is the founder's choice.

### 21. `makko_ho` — PASS WITH CORRECTIONS

- **Names and dates.** 真向法 まっこうほう; 長井津 ながい わたる (SSF; Kotobank/Kyodo). 1933 (昭和8年): the association's history page and Kotobank ("1933年から普及活動が始まった"). 1969 Ministry of Education authorisation and public-interest status: confirmed on the association site.
- **Origin.** Born at 勝鬘寺 in Fukui; stroke at 42; read the 勝鬘経, found the disciples' full bows (頭面接足礼, 五体投地礼), practised them, and after about three years his hips became supple. The association says Makkō-hō was not built on physiology or physical education. Confirmed.

### Method: per-step timing from the official video

I played the official video muted in a browser and saved still frames of the standing demonstrator every 1 s, and every 0.5 s around the step changes. From these I read when each movement starts: the first frame of its new pose (for example the arms crossing for step 2, the feet stepping apart for step 4, the first hop for step 11). No audio was analysed, stored or copied. Each boundary is accurate to about ±0.5 s.

The movements run from about 15 s to about 195 s: **about 180 s for the 200 counts**, which is roughly 0.9 s per count. This fits the Federation's "about 3 min 10 s" (190 s) with the intro, and Kampo's "about 3 minutes". The tempo is not even across the steps: the stretch and the hops are brisk, and steps 4 and 12 and the deep breath are slower.
