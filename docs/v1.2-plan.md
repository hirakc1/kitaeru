# v1.2 "First steps abroad": delivery plan

Owner: PM (Claude). Status: **approved, in progress** (2026-09-28). Streams A and B started. Scope and research come from `docs/world-movement.md` §7. This doc covers *how* v1.2 gets built and shipped.

## Scope (unchanged from the research roadmap)

There are 20 items from 4 traditions, plus rotation work.

| Group | Items |
|---|---|
| Radio Taisō | `radio_taiso_1` (flow, 13 moves, ~3 min), `rt_stretch_up`, `rt_trunk_twist`, `rt_side_bend` |
| Tai Chi | `taichi_commencement`, `taichi_cloud_hands`, `taichi_brush_knee`, `taichi_part_horse_mane`, `taichi_golden_rooster`, `taichi_short_flow` (~2.5 min) |
| Baduanjin | `baduanjin_hold_up_sky`, `baduanjin_draw_bow`, `baduanjin_look_back`, `baduanjin_sequence` (short version, ~6 min) |
| Strength traditions | `horse_stance`, `dand` (Hindu push-up), `baithak` (Hindu squat) |
| Rotation | `open_book`, `thread_the_needle`, `pallof_press` (+ `plank_shoulder_tap` fallback when there's no band) |

Platform work:
- the `sequence` schema and flow player;
- `traditions.js` with 5 culture cards;
- the breath guide (already built in anim v2);
- a balance block for 55+;
- a Morning Taisō toggle;
- `rotation` and `anti_rotation` families;
- Quick workout focus options `flow`, `balance` and `rotation`.

Radio Taisō No. 1 has 13 moves, but only 3 are standalone items in v1.2. The flow still needs every move animated, so the other 10 are built as flow-only steps and promoted to library items in v1.3.

## What we already have

- **Torso twist and head turn:** solved. The v2 rig has `yaw`, `twist` and `headYaw`, and the prototype already includes a Cloud Hands clip. That was the biggest animation risk in the research (§2.2 items 1 and 4).
- **Breath guide:** built as the "breath ring" and approved by the founder.
- **Countdown hold timer:** it gives the Tai Chi and stance holds their timing.

## What's still missing (critical path)

1. **Stepping with weight transfer.** The support foot switches mid-clip. Tai Chi (brush knee, horse mane, cloud hands side-steps) needs it, and so does anim batch 4 (conditioning), so it's built once for both. Estimate: about 1 week.
2. **Sequence chaining.** Flows are built by blending single clips together, with mirroring, rather than as one long clip.
3. **Hand shapes:** palm, fist, hook and point, for Tai Chi and Baduanjin.
4. **Practitioner review.** Per `world-movement.md` §5.1.7, **no tradition ships without a paid, credited reviewer.** This has the longest lead time and is outside our control, so it has to start now.

## Sequencing

```
Now ──────────────┬─ Anim batch 2 (in progress)
                  ├─ E: reviewer outreach + Kampo enquiry (founder)
                  └─ A: schema + data  →  B: planner  →  C: UI
After batch 2  ───── D: stepping/support-foot (also unblocks anim batch 4)
                     → v1.2 clips  ‖  batches 3 & 5 (core/skill, mobility) by a 2nd animator agent
Review lands ───── fix copy/cues/moves → founder review → ship v1.2
```

- Streams A–C run **in parallel with the 82-exercise conversion** and touch different files: data, engine and UI, while the animator works in `js/anim/`.
- A second animator agent takes batches 3 and 5, which don't need stepping, so the first animator can focus on stepping and the v1.2 clips. Batch 4 (conditioning) follows stepping.
- **Ship gate:** all 20 items converted, planner tests green, founder has reviewed them, and **every tradition has its reviewer sign-off**.
- **Fallback if a reviewer is late:** ship the platform plus the traditions that have been reviewed, and hide the rest behind `review` being unset. Rotation items are generic, so they need no cultural reviewer.

## Decisions (2026-09-28)
- **Balance goal:** yes.
- **Morning Taisō and the streak:** yes. It keeps the daily streak alive but doesn't count towards the weekly target.
- **Staffing:** yes. Streams A and B started now; C follows; a 2nd animator comes after batch 2.
- **Radio Taisō:**
  - User-facing name is "Morning Taisō". The "Radio Taisō No. 1" reference sits in a separate `attribution` field that can be blanked in one line.
  - The music is never used.
  - The trademark status of the name is unconfirmed: check it on J-PlatPat or ask Japan Post Insurance before using the name anywhere.
- **Reviewers: none (no budget).** Accuracy is based on sources instead:
  - each move is cross-checked against at least 2 independent sources, official or primary where they exist;
  - native names are checked against 2 sources;
  - an **independent fact-check agent** (not the author) sets `verified`;
  - then the founder reviews.
  - The gate hides a tradition until `verified` is set.
  - Cards show a "Sources" line, never "Reviewed by".
  - Stream E is now: the fact-check pass, plus a "Suggest a correction" link so practitioners can tell us about errors after launch.
